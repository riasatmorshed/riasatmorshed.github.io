## Just one rule to visualize
---
1. **$20 dB$ is a factor of 10**
2. Hence, $40 dB$ is a factor of 100
3. $3 dB$ is basically of factor $\sqrt2$  (But $-3 dB$ is a factor of $\frac{\sqrt2}{2}$)
4. $6 dB$ is a factor of $2$ (But $-6 dB$ is a factor of $0.5$ )
